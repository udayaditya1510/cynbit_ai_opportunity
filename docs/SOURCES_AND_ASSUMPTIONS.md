## How sources were used
The company website and public company profiles are used to understand publicly stated services, positioning, product names and programmes. Company-published descriptions are not treated as independently audited evidence of performance or product adoption.

## Important assumptions
1. The public service and product descriptions are current; confirm them with Cynbit before implementation.
2. Cynbit receives business enquiries through one or more digital channels; the exact intake volume and tools are unknown.
3. The company can lawfully access and process any historical enquiry data used for training or evaluation.
4. No internal CRM, analytics, support tickets, customer records, source code or product telemetry was accessed.
5. No quantitative revenue uplift, conversion improvement or time saving is claimed. These must be measured in a pilot.
6. The sample enquiries in `data/sample_leads.csv` are synthetic and do not represent real Cynbit customers.

## Research honesty note
This submission is an independent outside-in challenge project. Verify the current company structure, service catalogue, active products, preferred cloud provider, CRM, data governance requirements and internal priorities in a discovery discussion.
